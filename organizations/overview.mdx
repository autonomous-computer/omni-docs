An organization is the unit that owns TUROS resources: API keys, the wallet, webhook endpoints, stream subscriptions, monitors, and event history. Members share one billing context, so spend caps and budget alerts apply to everyone's work.

## Seats

Sandbox and Pay as you go include unlimited users. Team subscriptions include 5 seats. The Commercial plan extends multi-seat support with custom seat counts, dedicated infrastructure options, and contract-based invoicing.

Members authenticate to the TUROS dashboard through WorkOS. On enterprise, the same boundary extends to SSO - SAML and OIDC federation with Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and other identity providers - for customers who require corporate-IdP-backed login.

## Keys

API keys are org-scoped machine credentials, created from the TUROS dashboard and sent in the `x-api-key` header. The prefix is the mode: `secapi_test_…` is free and fixture-only, `secapi_live_…` bills successful work. The secret is shown once; the public `keyPrefix` is the first 20 characters.

Enterprise key management supports scopes: `read:sec`, `write:sec`, and `admin:operator`. Key limits follow the plan - Team includes 10 keys, Commercial includes 25.

An API key cannot create keys or change billing, and it cannot create or rotate webhook secrets or manage monitors. Those are dashboard-session actions. Keep that boundary in mind when you design agent access.

## Plans

| Plan key | Plan | What it sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sandbox_grant` | Sandbox | 100 included calls each month, renewing automatically. Test keys stay free. |
| `payg` | Pay as you go | Prepaid credits; enable from the dashboard. State becomes `payg_active`. |
| `personal` | Pro (`$49`/mo) | `$10` in included credits plus 15% discounted overages. |
| `team` | Team (`$199`/mo) | `$50` in included credits plus 25% discounted overages, 5 seats, 10 keys. |
| `commercial` | Commercial (from `$18,000`/yr) | Redistribution, embedding, white-label, resale, Bulk Factor Export, 25 keys. |

Self-serve plans cover evaluation and internal use only. Redistribution, embedding, white-labeling, resale, and Bulk Factor Export require Commercial - a successful request does not grant those rights. See [Billing and pricing](/admin/billing) for the full price list and meters.

## What the organization shares

- **Billing:** one wallet, shared spend caps, and budget alerts, with a single billing relationship.
- **Delivery:** shared webhook endpoints, stream subscriptions, and event history.
- **Audit:** the delivery ledger and usage reporting cover the whole organization.
