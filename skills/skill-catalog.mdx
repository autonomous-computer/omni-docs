# Skill Catalog

The following built-in skills are active in OMNI.

## Market and macro

- `market-data`: fast ticker and market context retrieval
- `macro-data`: FRED search and macro series analysis
- `upcoming-events`: earnings/dividend/split/IPO/economic calendars
- `read-news`: Perigon-backed news retrieval and summarization

## Company and industry research

- `company-primer`: investor-grade company brief
- `industry-primer`: market-structure and profit-pool analysis
- `competitors`: peer and substitute mapping
- `multi-company-analysis`: normalized multi-name comparison

## Earnings and estimates

- `earnings-preview`: pre-print expectation framing
- `earnings-recap`: post-print scorecard and reaction analysis
- `estimate-analysis`: consensus/targets/grades interpretation

## Portfolio, holdings, and flows

- `watchlists`: watchlist creation and maintenance
- `alerts`: inbox alert workflows
- `institutional-holdings`: 13F ownership intelligence
- `insider-trades`: insider transaction trend analysis
- `politician-stock-trades`: senate/house disclosure workflows

## Valuation and modeling

- `comps`: trading comparables workflow
- `dcf`: discounted cash flow framework
- `three-statement-model`: integrated IS/BS/CF structure
- `profit-and-loss-projection`: driver-based P&L forecasting
- `investment-thesis`: bull/bear thesis with catalysts and risks

## Documents and file analysis

- `pdf`: PDF extraction and analysis
- `docx`: DOCX extraction and analysis
- `xlsx`: spreadsheet extraction and analysis

## Platform and meta

- `skill-management`: discover and inspect skills
- `batch-fundamentals`: cross-ticker fundamentals comparison

## Notes

Skill metadata, endpoint contracts, and allowlists live in `packages/skills/` and are consumed by the agent backend at runtime.
