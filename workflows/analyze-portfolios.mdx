Explain what drove returns, measure factor exposures, test scenarios, and review risks through repeatable portfolio analysis. TUROS packages multi-step calculations behind explicit holdings, period, benchmark, and method inputs.

## Choose the job

- `POST /v1/portfolio/analyze` for a broad portfolio view.
- `POST /v1/portfolio/attribution` to explain returns by supported dimensions.
- `POST /v1/portfolio/risk` to review portfolio risk measures.
- `POST /v1/portfolio/stress-test` to apply a documented scenario.
- `POST /v1/portfolio/hedge` or `POST /v1/portfolio/optimize` for supported construction workflows.
- `POST /v1/factors/decomposition` and exposure endpoints for factor-specific questions.

## Make the analysis repeatable

1. Resolve holdings to stable security and company identities.
2. Record weights, effective date, currency, benchmark, and portfolio constraints.
3. Select a named analysis and inspect its required input schema.
4. Preserve methodology, assumptions, warnings, and missing-coverage fields.
5. Compare results only when the input and method definitions match.

## Review the boundary

Analysis is a model output, not a guarantee of performance or investment advice. A scenario is conditional, factor exposure is model-dependent, and attribution depends on the chosen benchmark and period. Keep those assumptions with the result.
