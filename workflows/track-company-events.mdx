Turn filing changes into a dated event stream for a company or watchlist. TUROS exposes structured events for areas such as M&A, offerings, auditor and officer changes, restatements, voting results, dilution, and corporate actions.

## Workflow

1. Resolve each company to a canonical entity.
2. Choose the event families and forms relevant to the investment question.
3. Backfill recent events and preserve their filing accessions and event dates.
4. Poll for changes or configure a monitor and delivery destination.
5. Reconcile every event with its source filing before taking action.

## Polling or delivery

Use polling for a small list or a worker that cannot receive public webhooks. Use [webhooks and streams](/events/configure) for durable delivery, retries, replay, and multiple event types. Monitor and delivery controls require a signed-in dashboard session; data reads use an API key.

## Keep time semantics explicit

Filed date, effective date, announced date, reporting period, and delivery time can differ. Preserve the date fields returned for the specific event instead of collapsing them into one timestamp.
