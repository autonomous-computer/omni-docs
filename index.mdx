# Welcome to OMNI

OMNI is a financial intelligence platform providing daily market briefings, AI-powered analysis, and portfolio tracking.

## What is OMNI?

OMNI delivers curated financial intelligence to help you stay informed about the markets that matter to you:

- **Daily Briefings** - Wake up to comprehensive market summaries
- **AI Analyst** - Chat with an AI trained on financial data
- **Portfolio Tracking** - Monitor your investments in real-time
- **Smart Alerts** - Get notified about market movements
