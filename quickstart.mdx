# Quickstart

Get up and running with OMNI in just a few steps.

## 1. Create an Account

Visit [omnibrief.app](https://omnibrief.app) and sign up with your Google account.

## 2. Explore Your First Briefing

Once logged in, you'll see today's market briefing on your dashboard. The briefing includes:

- Market overview and key movements
- Sector performance analysis
- Notable news and events
- Economic calendar highlights

## 3. Chat with the AI Analyst

Click the chat icon to open the OMNI Analyst. Try asking questions like:

- "What moved the markets today?"
- "Summarize the tech sector performance"
- "What are the key events this week?"

## 4. Set Up Alerts (Optional)

Navigate to the Alerts section to set up custom notifications:

- **Price alerts** - Get notified when securities hit target prices
- **News alerts** - Stay informed about specific topics or companies
- **Briefing alerts** - Receive daily briefing summaries via email
